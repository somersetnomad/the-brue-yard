Getting to Bruton

Plan Your Visit

Bruton has its own railway station, eight minutes’ walk from our gate. Most visitors arrive by train or on foot from the High Street.

By train 8 min walk

  • Signposted 8-minute foot walk from Bruton Railway Station.
  • GWR direct links to Westbury, Bath and Weymouth.
  • For London Paddington, change at Castle Cary (one stop).

On foot from the High Street

  • Walk down to the river and cross at Church Bridge.
  • Follow Station Road south, then turn left into Dropping Lane.
  • Pavements are level; the lane has one short slope at the gate.

By road & parking 4 EV bays

  • 4 accessible EV charging bays on site, beside the Welcome Desk.
  • Bicycle racks in the courtyard.
  • Overflow parking at Station Road, a 4-minute level walk.

Before you come

Questions

Can’t find what you need? Call the box office on 01749 555 018, Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00–17:00.

Do I need to book?

Booking a timed entry slot is the best way to be sure of getting in, especially at weekends. Walk-up tickets are sold at the Welcome Desk when there is space.

Are under 25s really free?

Yes. Under 25s and Somerset residents go free, but please reserve a ticket so we can manage numbers in the barns. Bring proof of age or a Somerset postcode.

Can I bring my dog?

Dogs on leads are welcome on the meadow trail and in the courtyard. Only assistance dogs can come into the barns.

Is there somewhere to eat?

The Granary Café serves breakfast, lunch and cake from 10:00 to 16:30, using produce from farms around Bruton. Picnics are welcome on the meadow.

What if it rains?

The barns are fully covered. The meadow paths are mown grass and can get muddy, so we lend wellies in most sizes at the desk.

Can I take photographs?

Yes, for personal use and without flash. A few loaned works are marked with a no-photography symbol.
