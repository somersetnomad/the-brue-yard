Support Us

Tickets cover less than half of what it costs to run the Yard. Friends, donors and funders pay for the rest, including free entry for everyone under 25.

Keep the rural school bus running

The nearest bus to the Yard from many villages runs twice a week. Our coach fund brings whole classes to Bruton for free.

Other ways to give

Trusts, businesses and legacies

  • Trusts and foundations

    Fund a season

    Our learning and access work is supported by trusts across the South West. Our director will gladly talk through what your grant could do.

  • Businesses

    Corporate partners

    Sponsor an exhibition, a family workshop series or the school bus fund, with staff visits and use of the Granary.

  • Legacies

    Leave a gift in your will

    A gift of any size helps keep entry free for young people. Email giving@brueyard.org.uk for a confidential conversation.
