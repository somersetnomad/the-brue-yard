For schools, families and the town

Learning & Community

Free visits for every Somerset state school, Saturday clay for families, and a monthly town forum in the Granary.

Schools

Free school visits

Every state school in Somerset can bring a class for free, including a two-hour workshop with an artist educator.

Our rural school bus fund pays for the coach from schools more than five miles away. Last year it brought 1,140 pupils from Wincanton, Castle Cary and Frome.

Workshops by key stage
EYFS & KS1Mud, Stone and Me
Sorting, stacking and printing with river stones.
KS2Mapping the Valley
Contour drawing on the meadow and a clay relief of the river.
KS3Casting Space
Plaster casting inspired by the Threshing Barn sculptures.
KS4–5Land and Making
Portfolio day for art and geography students, with a curator.

To book, email schools@brueyard.org.uk with your school, year group and preferred dates.

Community

For Bruton

  • First Tuesday, 18:30

    Town forum

    An open meeting in the Granary on what the Yard should do next. Tea provided, everyone welcome.

  • At cost

    Community Barn

    Bruton groups can hire the Community Barn for rehearsals, meetings and celebrations at cost. Venue hire

  • Thursdays

    Quiet sensory hour

    10:00–11:30, with lower light, no audio and a quiet room in the Granary.
