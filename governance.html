The charity

The Brue Yard Contemporary & Sculpture Meadows is a charitable incorporated organisation, registered with the Charity Commission for England and Wales, number 1189034.

Board of trustees

Nine trustees, at least three of whom live in Bruton or the surrounding parishes, serve terms of three years, renewable once. The board meets six times a year and publishes its minutes here.

Charity Governance Code

We follow the Charity Governance Code for smaller charities and review our practice against it every year, publishing what we have changed in our annual report.

Annual reports

Our annual report and accounts are published each January and filed with the Charity Commission.
