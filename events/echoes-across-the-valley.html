About this event

The exhibition fills the Threshing Barn and carries on outside along the North Meadow. Allow time for both: the meadow route is 1.2 km on mown paths and takes about 40 minutes at a gentle pace.

Timed entry keeps the Threshing Barn calm, so book a slot and stay as long as you like. The meadow is open until dusk, and the audio guide covers both the barn and the river path.
