About this event

Walk the river path at dusk with a guide, past all four commissions, then settle on the North Meadow for telescopes with Bruton Astronomical Society. If heavy rain cancels the walk, we email you by 15:00.

On a clear night the meadow is one of the darkest spots near Bruton. Expect Saturn, the Andromeda galaxy and, in December, the Geminid meteor shower. Hot drinks are served at the telescopes.
