The site

The grounds

Everything is within a few minutes’ walk of the Welcome Desk, and all three buildings are step-free.

  • Gallery 1 · Grade II

    The Threshing Barn

    The main exhibition hall, with its 1790s oak roof left open. Step-free throughout, with seating every 20 metres.

  • 1.2 km trail

    North Meadow & Pavilions

    Four timber pavilions and the riverside commissions. Mown paths, two gentle gradients, all-terrain chairs to borrow.

  • 120 seats

    The Community Barn

    Our performance space for Sunday matinees, folk nights and school assemblies. Hearing loop installed.
