Access for everyone

Accessibility Statement

We want everyone to be able to visit the Yard and use this website. This page covers both.

Visiting the Yard

All three buildings are step-free, with level thresholds and accessible toilets including a Changing Places room. The meadow trail is mown grass with two gentle slopes. All-terrain manual wheelchairs are free to borrow.

  • Carers and personal assistants come free.
  • Quiet sensory hour every Thursday, 10:00–11:30.
  • Visual stories, tactile large-print maps and ear defenders at the Welcome Desk.
  • Hearing loops in the Community Barn, Granary Room and at the desk.
  • BSL-interpreted family workshops every Saturday, and audio-described tours monthly.

This website

This website aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. You can:

  • use it with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus ring on every control
  • use it with screen readers, which announce filter results and ticket totals as they change
  • zoom to 400% without losing content
  • turn off animation by choosing reduced motion in your device settings
  • use it in light or dark mode

Known issues

Some photographs are stand-in images with shorter descriptions than we would like. We will replace them, with full descriptions, after our photography commission.

Tell us what doesn’t work

If something on the site or at the Yard gets in your way, email access@brueyard.org.uk or call 01749 555 018. We aim to reply within five working days.
