Exhibition essay · 8 min read

Reading the valley with your feet

By the curatorial team

The Brue drops some forty metres between the Bruton weir and the meadow’s eastern hedge. You feel that fall before you see it. Echoes Across the Valley begins from that sensation: sculpture as a way of measuring ground, and the ground as a record of every hand that has worked it.

In the Threshing Barn, cast forms hold the negative space of rooms and stairwells, set against the barn’s own worn threshing floor. Out on the North Meadow, walked lines and gathered stone answer the river’s contours. Four new commissions sit on the banks themselves, each placed where the flood line of 1917 once reached.
