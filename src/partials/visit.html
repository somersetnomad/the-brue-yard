Getting to Bruton

Plan Your Visit

Bruton has its own railway station, eight minutes’ walk from our gate. Most visitors arrive by train or on foot from the High Street.

By train 8 min walk

  • Signposted 8-minute foot walk from Bruton Railway Station.
  • GWR direct links to Westbury, Bath and Weymouth.
  • For London Paddington, change at Castle Cary (one stop).

On foot from the High Street

  • Walk down to the river and cross at Church Bridge.
  • Follow Station Road south, then turn left into Dropping Lane.
  • Pavements are level; the lane has one short slope at the gate.

By road & parking 4 EV bays

  • 4 accessible EV charging bays on site, beside the Welcome Desk.
  • Bicycle racks in the courtyard.
  • Overflow parking at Station Road, a 4-minute level walk.
