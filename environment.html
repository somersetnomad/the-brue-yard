Twelve acres to look after

Environmental Policy

We run a gallery in a floodplain meadow, so our footprint and the river’s health are the same question.

On this page

  1. Energy
  2. The meadow and river
  3. Travel
  4. Exhibitions

Energy

The barns are heated by ground-source heat pumps under the North Meadow, and the roof of the South Courtyard carries 60 kW of solar panels. We buy the rest from a renewable supplier.

The meadow and river

The North Meadow is cut once a year, in late summer, to keep its wildflowers. The wet meadow dug in 2024 slows floodwater above Bruton and has brought back water voles.

Travel

Most of our visitors’ carbon comes from getting here. We put train times first in every listing, give 10% off café orders for anyone with a same-day rail ticket, and provide secure bike racks.

Exhibitions

We reuse plinths and walls between shows, ship loans together where we can, and follow the Gallery Climate Coalition’s guidance.
