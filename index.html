    Exhibitions & Grounds

    A working farmyard turned gallery: two stone barns, a granary and a meadow trail that follows the river east of the town.

    • Gallery 1 · Grade II

      The Threshing Barn

      The main exhibition hall, with its 1790s oak roof left open. Step-free throughout, with seating every 20 metres.

    • 1.2 km trail

      North Meadow & Pavilions

      Four timber pavilions and the riverside commissions. Mown paths, two gentle gradients, all-terrain chairs to borrow.

    • 120 seats

      The Community Barn

      Our performance space for Sunday matinees, folk nights and school assemblies. Hearing loop installed.

    Exhibition essay · 8 min read

    Reading the valley with your feet

    By the curatorial team

    The Brue drops some forty metres between the Bruton weir and the meadow’s eastern hedge. You feel that fall before you see it. Echoes Across the Valley begins from that sensation: sculpture as a way of measuring ground, and the ground as a record of every hand that has worked it.

    In the Threshing Barn, cast forms hold the negative space of rooms and stairwells, set against the barn’s own worn threshing floor. Out on the North Meadow, walked lines and gathered stone answer the river’s contours. Four new commissions sit on the banks themselves, each placed where the flood line of 1917 once reached.

    By train 8 min walk

    • Signposted 8-minute foot walk from Bruton Railway Station.
    • GWR direct links to Westbury, Bath and Weymouth.
    • For London Paddington, change at Castle Cary (one stop).

    On foot from the High Street

    • Walk down to the river and cross at Church Bridge.
    • Follow Station Road south, then turn left into Dropping Lane.
    • Pavements are level; the lane has one short slope at the gate.

    By road & parking 4 EV bays

    • 4 accessible EV charging bays on site, beside the Welcome Desk.
    • Bicycle racks in the courtyard.
    • Overflow parking at Station Road, a 4-minute level walk.
