Twelve acres on the River Brue

Exhibitions & Grounds

A working farmyard turned gallery: two stone barns, a granary and a meadow trail that follows the river east of the town.

  • Gallery 1 · Grade II

    The Threshing Barn

    The main exhibition hall, with its 1790s oak roof left open. Step-free throughout, with seating every 20 metres.

  • 1.2 km trail

    North Meadow & Pavilions

    Four timber pavilions and the riverside commissions. Mown paths, two gentle gradients, all-terrain chairs to borrow.

  • 120 seats

    The Community Barn

    Our performance space for Sunday matinees, folk nights and school assemblies. Hearing loop installed.

Getting to Bruton

Plan Your Visit

Bruton has its own railway station, eight minutes’ walk from our gate.

  • Open

    Tuesday – Sunday

    10:00 – 17:00

    Meadow trail until dusk. Quiet sensory hour Thursdays 10:00–11:30.

  • By train

    8 minutes on foot

    Bruton station

    Direct GWR trains from Bath, Westbury and Weymouth. Change at Castle Cary for London.

  • Access

    Step-free barns

    All-terrain chairs

    Free to borrow for the meadow. Visual stories and tactile maps at the desk.

Schools, families and the town
